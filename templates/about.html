About VCH

What is VCH?

VCH is a virtual investment platform designed to help members grow their savings. Members can increase their earnings through activities such as vehicle repairs, virtual car rentals, and by referring other people to join the platform.

Please note: This is not a physical car hire service. It is entirely virtual and focuses solely on helping members save and grow their funds through referrals, repairs, and virtual rentals.

Choose from 8 luxury cars and earn daily income

2. Daily Servicing

3. Refer Friends

Earn 10% bonus + 2% daily commission

4. Become an Agent

Reach agent levels and earn monthly salary
